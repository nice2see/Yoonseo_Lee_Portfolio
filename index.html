My contribution

Applied the laboratory’s existing CT-OES framework; organised channel inputs and exclusions, reviewed Gaussian fitting and quality flags, and examined reconstructed emission fields and modelling assumptions.

What I learned

A good fit to measured projections does not establish an accurate spatial map. A same-forward-model, noiseless phantom showed 29.23% field error; temperature estimates remain model-conditional.

SUPPORTING PROJECT / MONITORING EVALUATION

False alarms and missed events

I extended the diagnostic work into controlled synthetic PCA/SVM comparisons and correlated-noise tests. In a separate public N₂ study, I examined calibration and the cost of withholding alarms.

50.00% → 13.56%

Mean false-positive rate after retrospective, label-assisted normal-reference recalibration on matched future frames. Three physical sessions were reused in six roles; this was not blind transfer or production-fab validation.

Adding a further guard lowered false alarms but reduced unconditional recall from 99.39% to 52.56%. I retained the guard as a review signal rather than an automatic veto.

Public N₂ source: Youngjun Yuk, Mendeley Data v1 (CC BY 4.0). The N₂ study is separate from the synthetic Ar evaluation.

Method references

These references document the research methods and historical atomic-data lineage. Current demo results come from the later portfolio implementation; the earlier research notebooks are not substituted for that implementation.

  1. Schulze et al. (2008), J. Phys. D 41, 065206
    Common-upper line ratios and escape-factor methodology.
  2. Chouteau et al. (2024), PSST 33, 075016
    Eq. (8): quasi-steady metastable balance. Eqs. (10)-(11): relative electron-density interpretation. Project assumptions and inputs differ.
  3. Donnelly (2004), J. Phys. D 37, R217
    Historical SNU input: Fig. 5 thick direct-plus-cascade curve, distinct from measured apparent symbols. It is not the later demo BSR input.
  4. Dasgupta, Blaha & Giuliani (1999), Phys. Rev. A 61, 012703
    Historical 1s5-to-2p1...2p10 input: Fig. 6 solid theoretical curves, on collision energy. Published 9 December 1999.
  5. Boffard et al. (2012), J. Phys. D 45, 045201
    Osaka 420.1/419.8 nm method; not applied to the supplied SNU spectral range.
  6. Youngjun Yuk, Mendeley Data v1
    Separate public measured N2 dataset, CC BY 4.0. The reported calibration figures are saved evaluation aggregates, not new experiments.

Donnelly Fig. 5 primary lineage: Schappe et al., Phys. Rev. A 50, 444 (1994), DOI 10.1103/PhysRevA.50.444; Chutjian & Cartwright, Phys. Rev. A 23, 2178 (1981), DOI 10.1103/PhysRevA.23.2178. Literature describes the method; it does not independently validate this project’s numerical results.
